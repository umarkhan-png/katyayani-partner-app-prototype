⚠️ Check before you publish
  • • Remove any line for a feature not shipping in this build (Screen Share, Price Match, Advisor cart, Credit) — Play removes listings for features that don't exist in the app.
  • • Credit lines (“pay in 30 days”, “no CIBIL problem on delay”, “0% interest”) need legal / compliance sign-off before going live.
  • • Screenshots carry sample data (shop name, ₹ limits, farmer names). Fine for a store listing, but keep them consistent with what a new user actually sees.
